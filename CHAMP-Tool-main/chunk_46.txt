Stockholm’s STOLT system demonstrator
 is advancing the city’s ambition for an emission-free inner city by 2030 by using the introduction of a Class 3 Environmental 
Zone and the Urban Environment Zone in Gamla Stan as strategic windows of opportunity for wider transport-system transformation. Rather than testing a single solution, STOLT coordinates a broad ecosystem of city departments, businesses, research and innovation actors, infrastructure stakeholders and civil society around a portfolio that includes stakeholder dialogue, fossil-free transport procurement, electrified construction, off-peak logistics and emerging concepts such as co-loading hubs. This coordination is particularly significant in Stockholm’s complex institutional setting
 – 
the demonstrator has succeeded in working across municipal departments and external stakeholders through recurring coordination, shared portfolio oversight and continuous adaptation. The result is not only a set of demonstrations, but an implementation environment that helps maintain a common direction, surface regulatory and operational bottlenecks, and connect learning across activities so that successful approaches can be institutionalized and replicated beyond the original testbeds.
