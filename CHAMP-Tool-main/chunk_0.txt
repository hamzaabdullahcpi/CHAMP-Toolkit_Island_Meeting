Cities face an estimated USD 4.3 trillion annual urban climate financing gap to 2030 for mitigation alone. To this end, the Toolkit aims to empower CHAMP countries in delivering national climate commitments at the city level – where emissions and risks are concentrated – by strengthening the multilevel governance and investment systems needed to implement urban climate action and mobilize 
and redirect
 
finance. 
More specifically, the Toolkit reflects and responds to the following challenges: 
Although national climate commitments, such as NDCs, are increasingly recognizing the role of cities, financing and implementation of urban climate action
 at sufficient scale and speed
 remain a challenge
Limited coordination between national and local governments, capacity gaps, and financing challenges continue to hinder the translation of urban priorities into investible pipelines and project
 portfolios
 at scale
While a range of implementation-focused resources and tools exist, they remain fragmented and not easily accessible to nor actionable 
for
