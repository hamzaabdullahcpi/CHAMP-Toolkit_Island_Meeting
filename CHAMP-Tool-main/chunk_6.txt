Action
 2: Assess Enabling Environments
This Action will provide foundational assessment tools to identify and improve the policy, fiscal, data and coordination conditions needed for national, subnational and city governments to plan, access and deploy urban climate finance. It will help users diagnose barriers to climate investment readiness, including gaps in financing architecture, intergovernmental transfers, subnational borrowing, data systems, coordination, private finance mobilization and project bankability.
