LoCAL
 is a country-based mechanism
 spearheaded by the 
UN Capital Development Fund (UNCDF)
 
It 
helps local governments integrate climate adaptation into planning and budgeting
 through 
performance-based climate resilience grants
 
and
 technical and capacity-building support.
 It channels climate finance
 to local governments
 through existing
 national
 fiscal transfer systems. In practice, local governments assess risks and needs, prepare adaptation plans, 
identify
 and 
budget
 priority measures, and implement them through their annual budgeting cycles; performance reviews and audits then inform future allocations. LoCAL is designed to scale in phases, moving from piloting to learning and then to full national roll-out
, which makes it a strong example of a multilevel local climate finance structure embedded in national systems.
