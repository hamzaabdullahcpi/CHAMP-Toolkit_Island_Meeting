Systems Logic:
 Multilevel climate action starts with shared political commitment. Joint commitment processes can create a common mission, clarify roles across governance levels, and build the mandate, trust and institutional relationships needed for later stages of investment planning, finance mobilization, implementation and learning. In practice, they help move climate action from isolated pledges toward coordinated, network-based governance.
