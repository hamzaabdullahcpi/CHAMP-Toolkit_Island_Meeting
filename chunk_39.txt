The Fund aims to partner with key global funders and implementation actors to deploy EUR 10 million by 2030 in co-financing and another EUR 10 million in aggregated demand through a focus on climate-aligned procurement.
Viability Fund’s support offer
The Viability Fund offers cities two core forms of support. First, catalytic co-financing: through its Co-finance pillar, the Fund 
will contribute
 roughly 10 percent of the capital needed for a local investment, taking an early, credibility-anchoring position that is then topped up through national and international co-financing dialogues rather than covering the full cost itself. Second, a local investment fund as a service: because a city must establish its own local climate investment fund, built on shared principles, to receive the Fund's co-financing, the Fund helps set up and run that vehicle, potentially through a fund-manager partner, so it can channel capital to local stakeholders such as solar, batteries, energy infrastructure and SMEs, and recycle returns into harder-to-finance areas like wetlands. Alongside both sits governance and coordination support: aligning funders around common grant principles without pooling capital, and helping cities connect governance, investment planning and procurement — reflecting the Fund's guiding logic that finance follows governance.

Urban Infrastructure Insurance Facility (UIIF
)
 
is a
 
multilevel 
insurance-based risk pooling mechanism 
involving municipal governments,
 international donor agencies and 
city networks. It demonstrates
 how cities 
can address 
climate and disaster exposure
 risks
 and protect critical infrastructure. In the UIIF model, ten cities in Latin America and the Caribbean undergo risk assessments that inform tailored insurance coverage and disaster risk reduction measures, with individual city risks transferred into a pooled structure that reduces premiums through economies of scale and risk sharing. After a disaster, cities can access financing more quickly to rebuild infrastructure and support vulnerable residents, while also benefiting from better risk management, legal and technical support, and stronger relationships with financial institutions.
