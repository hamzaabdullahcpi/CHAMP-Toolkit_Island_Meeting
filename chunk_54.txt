The model aimed 
to scale up the deployment of e-buses in India
 and

Led by 
Convergence Energy Service Limited (CESL)
 under the Ministry of Power, and supported by TUMI E-bus Mission, the 
model
 
pooled
 demand from multiple cities for e-buses, reduced transaction costs through a single tendering process and used subsidies from the national government, with some state contributions as well. 
CESL first invited cities to estimate demand and share route, depot and charging information, then consolidated those inputs into a standardized pooled tender. Before issuing the procurement, CESL also consulted OEMs through informal discussions and an initial expression of interest, which helped it test market appetite and adjust the tender design. The tender was then split into five lots with different technical specifications, and bids were selected

kilometre.
