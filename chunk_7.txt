Why it is needed:
 Multilevel climate investment depends on enabling conditions being in place across data, policy, budget and finance, and coordination — yet these are often weak, uneven or invisible to decision-makers, leaving national, subnational and city governments unsure where the readiness gaps lie. To this end, enabling environment assessments surface where those conditions fall short, consolidate them into a comparable readiness profile, and channel the findings into concrete reforms, so the diagnostic feeds directly into investment planning, finance mobilisation and implementation rather than remaining a standalone report.
Key actors:
 National and subnational governments lead the assessment, supported where needed by technical partners, project preparation facilities, city networks and NGOs.
